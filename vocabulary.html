📖 Self Introduction Vocabulary

Learn useful English words for introducing yourself!

👋 Basic Information

NAME

Nama

Example: My name is Anna.

AGE

Umur

Example: I am 10 years old.

HOMETOWN

Kota asal

Example: My hometown is Singaraja.

COUNTRY

Negara

Example: I am from Indonesia.

🎨 Hobbies

DRAWING

Menggambar

Example: My hobby is drawing.

READING

Membaca

Example: I like reading books.

PLAYING FOOTBALL

Bermain sepak bola

Example: I like playing football.

SWIMMING

Berenang

Example: I like swimming.

SINGING

Menyanyi

Example: My hobby is singing.

DANCING

Menari

Example: I like dancing.

⭐ Favorite Things

FAVORITE COLOR

Warna favorit

Example: My favorite color is blue.

FAVORITE FOOD

Makanan favorit

Example: My favorite food is pizza.

FAVORITE ANIMAL

Hewan favorit

Example: My favorite animal is a cat.

FAVORITE SUBJECT

Mata pelajaran favorit

Example: My favorite subject is English.

💡 Useful Words

LIKE

Suka

Example: I like reading.

LOVE

Sangat suka

Example: I love music.

LIVE

Tinggal

Example: I live in Bali.

FROM

Dari

Example: I am from Bali.
